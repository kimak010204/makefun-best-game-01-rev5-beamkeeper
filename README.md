🔦 **BEAMKEEPER — Light the Braziers, Hold the Night.** A light puzzle-defense game for the make.fun best-game-01 bounty. Across five nights, a golden beam from the lantern on the left must be bent through mirrors across a 7×7 board to light all three braziers before dawn — and that same beam burns the shadow wisps creeping in from the right toward your lantern. Click a mirror to rotate it 90°, route the beam around the board, and spend EMBER earned from burned wisps on boons between nights. Win by surviving all five nights; lose if the lantern's flames go out, or dawn arrives with a brazier still dark.
